## 2. Terms of Service

By creating an account, users agree to:

- Provide accurate, current, and complete registration information
- Maintain the security of their password and account
- Accept responsibility for all activities under their account
- Notify Connecta immediately of unauthorized use

### 2.2 Age Requirement

- Minimum age: **18 years** (or age of majority in jurisdiction)
- Age verified at registration via date of birth entry

- Solicit money, gifts, or financial information from other users

### 2.5 Intellectual Property

- Connecta retains all rights to the platform, branding, and codebase
- Users retain ownership of content they create (photos, messages, profile)
- Users grant Connecta a limited, non-exclusive license to display their content on the platform

## 4. Data Protection

### 4.1 Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR)

- Data processing must have a lawful basis (consent, contract, legal obligation, legitimate interest)
- Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) required for high-risk processing
- Appointment of a Data Protection Officer (DPO) required
- Data breach notification to NDPC within 72 hours
- Cross-border data transfers require adequacy assessment

### 4.2 Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023

The NDPA 2023 supersedes the NDPR and introduces:
- Expanded definition of personal data
- Mandatory registration of data controllers and processors
- Enhanced penalties for non-compliance
- Right to data portability
- Regulation of automated decision-making

**Compliance Actions:**
- Register as data controller with NDPC
- Conduct annual data protection audits
- Implement privacy by design in all new features
- Maintain records of processing activities (ROPA)

### 4.3 GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

For users accessing the platform from the EU/EEA:
- Lawful basis for processing documented for all activities
- Explicit consent for marketing communications
- Data Protection Impact Assessment for AI matchmaking
- Data Protection Officer contact: dpo@connecta.ng
- EU representative (if required): To be appointed before EU launch

### 4.4 Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)

Required with all third-party processors:
- Cloud hosting providers (AWS, GCP, Azure)
- Payment processors (Paystack, Flutterwave)
- Analytics providers
- Customer support tools
- Email/SMS notification services

Each DPA must include:
- Purpose and scope of processing
- Security measures implemented
- Sub-processor management
- Breach notification obligations
- Data return/deletion upon termination

## 5. Consent Management

### 5.1 Age Verification

- Date of birth required at registration
- System rejects users under 18
- No parental consent mechanism — under-18 users are prohibited
- Periodic audits to detect false age declarations

### 5.2 Consent Collection

| Consent Type | Collection Method | Granularity |
|-------------|-------------------|-------------|
| Account creation | Registration form | Full account terms accepted |
| Profile visibility | Privacy settings | Per-profile-element control |
| Location sharing | In-app prompt | Granular (distance range, exact location) |
| Push notifications | OS-level prompt | Category-specific (matches, messages, promotions) |
| Marketing emails | Opt-in checkbox | Separate from account terms |
| Data processing | Consent modal | Per-purpose granularity |

### 5.3 Opt-in Marketing

- Marketing consent is **separate** from service terms
- Pre-checked boxes are prohibited (GDPR compliance)
- Unsubscribe link in every marketing communication
- Consent records maintained with timestamp and IP

### 5.4 Data Processing Consent

- Consent collected before first data processing activity
- Consent is informed (clear explanation of what is processed and why)
- Consent is freely given (not bundled with account creation)
- Consent is specific (per-purpose, not blanket)
- Consent is unambiguous (affirmative action required)

### 5.5 Consent Withdrawal

- Withdrawal available via Privacy Settings screen
- Same ease as giving consent (one-tap withdrawal)
- Withdrawal does not affect lawfulness of prior processing
- System stops processing within 24 hours of withdrawal

## 6. Content Guidelines

### 6.1 Acceptable Content

- Profile photos showing a clear face
- Genuine, accurate profile descriptions
- Respectful, consensual messages
- Appropriate content as defined by community standards

### 6.2 Prohibited Content

| Category | Examples | Consequence |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Explicit sexual content | Nude photos, sexual messages | Immediate removal, account ban |
| Violence | Threats, graphic content | Immediate removal, account ban, law enforcement referral |
| Harassment | Unwanted messages, stalking, bullying | Warning → suspension → ban |
| Hate speech | Discrimination, slurs, dehumanization | Immediate removal, account ban |
| Illegal activity | Drug trafficking, prostitution solicitation | Immediate removal, law enforcement referral |
| Fraud | Fake identities, financial scams | Immediate removal, permanent ban |
| Intellectual property infringement | Unauthorized use of others' photos | Removal, account suspension |

### 6.3 Reporting Mechanism

- **In-app reporting:** Report button on every profile and message
- **Report categories:** Inappropriate photos, fake profile, scam, harassment, other
- **Priority levels:** Emergency (imminent harm) → High → Medium → Low
- **Response time:** Emergency: < 1 hour | High: < 24 hours | Medium: < 72 hours

### 6.4 Moderation Process

1. User submits report
2. Automated triage (keyword detection, pattern matching)
3. Human moderator review
4. Action taken (warning, content removal, suspension, ban)
5. Reporter notified of outcome
6. Reported user notified with appeal instructions
7. All actions logged for audit trail

## 7. Safety Policies

### 7.1 Anti-Harassment Policy

- Zero tolerance for harassment, stalking, or intimidation
- Block and report features prominently accessible
- Automated detection of sending patterns (mass messaging, rapid swiping)
- Restricted messaging for new accounts (limited messages per day)
- Keyword filters for known harassment patterns

### 7.2 Romance Scam Prevention

- **AI scam detection:** Regex + behavioral pattern matching (see Doc 22)
- **Red flag indicators:** Rapid requests for money, love-bombing, sob stories, off-platform communication requests
- **User education:** In-app safety tips, scam awareness notifications
- **Reporting shortcut:** "Report Scam" button in chat with priority queue
- **Financial protection:** No in-app money transfer between users

### 7.3 Fake Profile Detection

- Photo verification (optional, incentivized)
- Behavioral analysis for bot-like patterns
- IP and device fingerprint monitoring for multiple accounts
- Manual review for flagged accounts
- Community reporting for suspicious profiles

### 7.4 Emergency Contacts

- SOS button accessible from all screens
- Integration with Nigerian emergency services (112, 767, 199)
- Share location feature for date meetups
- Trusted contact notification feature

### 7.5 Safety Tips

- Never send money to someone you haven't met in person
- Meet in public places for first dates
- Tell a friend or family member about your plans
- Trust your instincts — if something feels wrong, leave
- Report suspicious behavior immediately

## 8. Payment Compliance

### 8.1 PCI DSS Awareness

Connecta does **not** store, process, or transmit card data directly. All payment processing is handled by PCI DSS-compliant third-party processors.

- Card data tokenized at the client level
- Payment forms hosted by the processor (not embedded in Connecta)
- No card numbers in Connecta databases or logs

### 8.2 Payment Processor Compliance

| Processor | Use Case | Compliance |
|-----------|----------|------------|
| Paystack | Card payments, bank transfers | PCI DSS Level 1, NDPR compliant |
| Flutterwave | Multi-channel payments | PCI DSS Level 1, NDPR compliant |

- Processor DPAs on file
- Webhook signatures verified for all payment notifications
- Idempotent payment processing to prevent double charges

### 8.3 Refund Policy

- **Subscription refunds:** Prorated refund within 14 days of purchase if no premium features used
- **Accidental purchases:** Full refund within 72 hours upon request
- **Billing disputes:** Resolution within 5 business days
- **No refund** for suspended/banned accounts (terms violation)

### 8.4 Subscription Terms

- Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled
- Price changes communicated 30 days in advance
- Cancellation effective at end of current billing period
- Free trial conversion requires explicit opt-in
- Cancellation available via in-app settings (no call required)

## 11. DMCA / Copyright

### 11.1 Copyright Infringement Reporting

DMCA-style takedown requests can be submitted via:
- In-app report (for profile photos)
- Email: dmca@connecta.ng
- Mail: Connecta Technologies Limited (address TBD)

Required information:
- Identification of copyrighted work
- Identification of infringing material
- Contact information of complainant
- Good faith statement
- Accuracy statement under penalty of perjury

### 11.2 Repeat Infringer Policy

- First infringement: Content removal + warning
- Second infringement: 30-day account suspension
- Third infringement: Permanent account termination
- Counter-notification process available

### 11.3 Counter-Notification

Users whose content is removed may submit a counter-notification including:
- Identification of removed material
- Statement of good faith belief
- Consent to jurisdiction
- Signature (physical or electronic)
